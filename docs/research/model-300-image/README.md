## Decision and remaining gap

No Model 300 image is copied, downloaded, embedded or hotlinked. The standalone component displays a neutral message and links to the manufacturer's product page. Public visibility and a media-kit link do not settle rights for the gallery. The photographer or actual rights holder, licence or explicit permission covering this independent public demo, allowed transformations, attribution wording, and capture/configuration details remain unknown. The source check is a research review, not a legal clearance.

**Next action, owner: product owner / issue #199.** Obtain a written grant or a clearly applicable Model 300 image licence from the relevant rights holder, without sending a request unless authorized. Record the image identity, permission terms and attribution, then supply a local asset and metadata to `ModelImage`. Until then the fallback can support the demo, but the photo acceptance criterion remains open. Controlled Model 300 specifications and accepted site/rule evaluation have separate evidence gates.
