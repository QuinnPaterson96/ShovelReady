# Municipal fees: what can be calculated and what still needs review

Checked October 16, 2026 UTC. This review concerns hypothetical detached ADUs, not charges payable for a real address. Source IDs and retrieval limits resolve to the [official source matrix](sources.md). All dollar fee figures below are Canadian dollars. Rates are source observations, and eligibility statements are preliminary interpretations requiring review before execution.

**A reliable fee feature needs two separate answers: does this charging event apply, and how is its amount calculated?** An unknown exemption, application date, legal area, prior payment or service scope must leave an unresolved line. Never make it zero. Refundable securities belong in cash required, separate from non-refundable capital cost; a creditable application payment must not be added twice.

## Victoria: temporary waiver before base-rate arithmetic

The [base bylaw, Schedule A](https://www.victoria.ca/media/file/building-and-plumbing-regulation-bylaw-17-113-consolidated#page=22) and [August 2026 guide, p1](https://www.victoria.ca/media/file/guide-building-permit-fees-and-deposits#page=1) show a $100 application fee and a building fee of 1.4% of construction cost excluding plumbing/electrical values. Professional/affordable-housing reductions have conditions. **Those base rates are not a current payable quote.**

[Bylaw26-020, ss2–6](https://www.victoria.ca/media/4426) sets listed building/plumbing, land-use and other fees to zero for qualifying complete applications received after adoption on January 29, 2026 through repeal 12 months later. “Received” requires City acceptance and excludes determined/rejected/withdrawn applications. Read the actual s4 list: it does **not** establish a waiver of DCCs, ACCs, utility construction, deposits or private professional work. Store receipt/status and fee identity, not merely “project year2026.” The source is explicit; its application to a real submission still needs confirmation.

## Saanich: application credit is essential

[Building Bylaw9529, Part21 Appendix A p52](https://www.saanich.ca/assets/Local~Government/Documents/Bylaws~and~Policies/BUILDING%20BYLAW%2C%202019%2C%20NO.%209529.pdf#page=52) states $100 for the first $1,000, $13 per $1,000 for the next $499,000 and $10 per $1,000 above $500,000. The application payment **forms part of** the permit fee. In the $100,001–$500,000 band it is $1,000. Moving a building includes building value plus site work. Plumbing quantities and s10.8 security classification remain separate.

**One bounded worked calculation:** assume a Saanich building official accepts **$200,000** as the complete permit valuation, wholly within the relevant tier, with no reduction or penalty. This is an explicit arithmetic assumption, not a manufacturer price or assessed value. Then:

`$100 + (199 × $13) = $2,687 total building-permit fee.`

`$1,000 application payment + $1,687 remaining permit payment = $2,687.`

Plumbing, deposits, development applications, utility works, regional charges and private costs are excluded and remain unresolved. The accepted valuation and circumstances would be required to use this result for any real project. Whole-thousand input intentionally avoids an unresolved rounding interpretation.

The [Q&A](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/local-government/inspections-services/permit-q-as.html) says “per thousand or part,” while p52's schedule omits those words. Application bands also leave literal exact-boundary gaps such as $15,000. Resolve before encoding; do not silently invent rounding or repair the text.

For development/servicing charges, [DCC9553 definitions and Parts2–4](https://www.saanich.ca/assets/Local~Government/Documents/Bylaws~and~Policies/DEVELOPMENTCOSTCHARGESBYLAW9553.pdf) treat a garden/secondary suite as not a separate unit for “Single Family,” but also impose charges on fewer-than-four-unit buildings and have exemptions/previous-payment conditions. This is not sufficient to announce every ADU exempt. Exemptions include each unit ≤29m² residential-only and work ≤$50,000; water/sewer service areas and extra catchments affect the applicable schedule. The retrieved consolidation includes amendments9881/9987 and phased tables. Latest operative rate/effective date still needs reconciliation.

The official [connection-fee page](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/local-government/departments/engineering-department/service-connection-fees-additional-charges.html) intermittently served an election notice; no complete current connection-rate schedule was accepted here. Existing service size/capacity, proposed work and municipal estimate are missing. Other development permits/variances depend on the parcel and proposed work; no generic application bundle is assumed. Decision: this permit-component arithmetic is the best inexpensive review experiment; it is not a complete project-fee engine.

## Langford: calculator exists, but wasn't tested

[Building Bylaw1160 Appendix A pp23–24](https://langford.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/1160-Building-Bylaw-2008-Consolidated-to-20260601-1.pdf#page=23), consolidated June 1, 2026, sets $63.41 for the first $1,000, then $12.66/thousand or part for the next $99,000, $10.18 for the next $900,000, $5.12 for the next $14million and $2.58 above $15million. Non-refundable application payment is included: $123.22 at valuation ≤$200,000; $1,196.97 above it. Valuation is the greater of contract including taxes/extras or the inspector's estimate. Professional certification can reduce fees5%; plumbing is separate ($19.15/fixture). The official [calculator](https://feecalculators.langford.ca/) timed out, so no parity claim is made.

The [combined fee packet pp1–2](https://langford.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Fees-Combined-for-Website-20250317.pdf#page=1) dates planning fees to May 1, 2026, corrected August 25. Its table lists garden/carriage suites in the $660 form-and-character band, with environmentally sensitive/hazardous-area comparison and different fire/riparian-only paths. Do not treat $660 as an unconditional ADU permit fee. Planning fees have annual CPI adjustment language. The packet's DCC sheets are dated May 14, 2025, not the same revision as planning fees.

Roads, parks, north/south storm catchments, sewer/storage and outside-agency charges require separate applicability. [Parks bylaw2024 ss2–6](https://langford.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Parks-DCC-Bylaw.pdf) explicitly reaches fewer-than-four-unit residential buildings, contains prior-charge credits and a ≤$50,000 work exemption; its “townhouse” definition also includes detached dwellings on one lot. This illustrates why a colloquial “garden suite” label cannot select the cheapest schedule row. The [water/sewer page](https://langford.ca/residents/resident-resources/water-sewer/) and packet identify external responsibilities; municipal plumbing-connection permit entries are not the physical connection works price.

Decision: provide calculator link/manual fallback; deterministic base-fee arithmetic is feasible after valuation and current-rate review. DCC catchment/classification, utility works and credits require manual review; no accepted Langford total produced.

## Oak Bay: current base schedule is an explicit access gap

The current 4845 building-fee consolidation could not be retrieved successfully; an indexed version ends March 11, 2024. **No current base building-permit amount or formula is adopted.** The older [Building/Plumbing4247](https://www.oakbay.ca/sites/default/files/municipal-hall/4247-%20%20Building%20and%20Plumbing%20Bylaw%20-%20Consolidated%20to%204595.pdf) points to the fees bylaw and contains repealed/struck historical fee text. Do not resurrect its historical percentage/application credit. The official [plumbing application](https://www.oakbay.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Plumbing-Permit-Application.pdf) lists a $50 base plus scope-dependent charges and warns of possible meter upgrade; it is not a current full-rate schedule.

[Amendment4952 attachment pp9–11](https://oakbay.civicweb.net/document/167207/Planning,%20Building%20and%20Development%20Fees%20and%20Cha.pdf?handle=6DC5C1679ED64EB0A01A914736E7C855#page=9) replaces **development-application Schedule1**, not the building schedule. [May 11 minutes resolution226](https://oakbay.civicweb.net/document/175333/) confirm adoption. Examples: delegated infill DP $1,500; DVP $1,750; delegated minor DVP $1,000; DP with variances adds $750. Applicability and final current text still require review. [July 27 minutes resolution376](https://oakbay.civicweb.net/document/179079/) adopt4961 for blasting fees: the older consolidation is demonstrably insufficient.

[DCC4891 Parts2–5](https://www.oakbay.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/4891-Development-Cost-Charge-Bylaw-2024.pdf) includes ADUs in overlapping low/medium development descriptions, explicitly reaches fewer-than-four-unit projects, and provides ≤29m² residential-only, ≤$50,000-work and prior-payment exemptions. Schedule A totals are $26,187 low and $16,747 medium, **not a classification of the hypothetical ADU**. DCC adopted February 10, 2025. Obtain an applicability interpretation before choosing a row.

[ACC4892 Parts2–5 and Schedule A](https://www.oakbay.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/4892-Amenity-Cost-Charge-Bylaw.pdf) lists $9,465 low/$6,441 medium; population/affordable-housing/prior-payment conditions differ from DCC. No automatic29m² ACC exemption is established. The PDF prints adoption January 13,**2024** after December 2024 readings; the [official program page](https://connect.oakbay.ca/dcc-acc) says January 2025. Record this date conflict and request the authoritative record before executable use. Current engineering/water/sewer rates and work scope remain missing. Decision: manual source review; no Oak Bay automatic total yet.

## Regional and utility authorities are separate

CRD's [September 10, 2026 adoption notice](https://www.crd.ca/news/regional-water-supply-service-development-cost-charge-bylaw-approved) establishes RWS Bylaw4658 adoption September 9, delayed effectiveness April 2, 2027 and eligible in-stream protection until September 9, 2027. Its [FAQ](https://getinvolved.crd.bc.ca/water-supply-dcc/widgets/180428/faqs) describes detached suites as medium density, $7,914/unit, distinct from attached suites and existing DCC programs. It covers all four target municipalities where serviced. This is a **future conditional exposure**, not a charge payable simply because this report was captured in2026. Final bylaw exemptions and actual precursor/issuance dates require review; no assumption of eligibility or payable amount is made.

Langford's packet separately lists Juan de Fuca distribution DCCs, sewer-related charges and School District62 acquisition charges. Do not substitute that distribution schedule for regional RWS or apply SD62 charges to every municipality. Confirm service area, charging event, net additional units and exemptions with the governing authority. Likewise, [BC Hydro's connection schedule](https://app.bchydro.com/accounts-billing/electrical-connections/connection-requests/express/fees-charges.html) concerns the utility portion; private excavation, wiring and panel work need their own scope. Technical Safety BC/municipal electrical-permit jurisdiction, gas, septic/Island Health and other authority costs remain unresolved where applicable.

## Proposed calculation boundary

Calculate an individual fee only after a reviewer supplies jurisdiction, application/receipt/issue date, authoritative fee revision, accepted valuation or defined legal area, use/units, applicable overlays, prior-charge credit and exemption evidence. Store the calculation inputs and source locator beside the result. Preserve base amount, waiver/reduction and net amount separately. At-cost work, ambiguous classification or unknown exemption remains **needs review**; a missing quote remains **unknown**. Neither has a numerical default.

A reviewer should next verify the Saanich example against the operative bylaw and intended project class; reconcile Victoria's DCC rate discrepancy; acquire current Oak Bay4845/utility schedules; and confirm Langford calculator parity. This bounded research has not certified any municipality's complete charging regime.
