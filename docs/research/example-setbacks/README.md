**Line classification and projections.** Part 2.1 defines Corner Lot (PDF pp14–15), Front Lot Line (p16), Lot Line (p18), Rear Lot Line, Side Lot Line, Flanking Street Lot Line and Setback (p19). A corner lot's front line can depend on ground-access unit frontage or the shortest street edge; another lot with two or more street edges can depend on wider-street frontage. A triangular lot can have a *point* deemed its rear line. A four-sided GIS polygon does not prove the site is interior, corner, through or free of an easement. Part 1.1(12), PDF p8, and GRD-1 Part 4.1(4)(e), p32, address permitted projections; the latter lists eaves up to **0.75 m** and other specified features with separate limits. Part 2.1 Setback excludes cornices, retaining walls and fences, and up to **0.13 m** of listed exterior wall treatment/insulation/rainscreen. These are feature-specific rules, **not** a generic allowance to shrink the suite or subtract 0.75 m from every threshold. Obtain a controlled projection schedule and review the interaction with the garden-suite-specific clause before any comparison.

Confirm the adopted amendment chain through the evaluation date, including any parcel-specific zoning or variance. Part 1.1(21), PDF pp9–10, preserves certain recent permits, variance orders and transition applications; check parcel-specific approvals rather than assuming the base text is exhaustive. No site-specific entry for “1144 May” was found by searching this PDF, which is not proof that none applies.

**Illustrative display is supportable now:** retain the existing EPSG:3157 nominal placement and report geometric parcel/roofline distances as such. With the present `named_boundaries: []`, display no per-edge legal threshold or pass/fail; a user-supplied exploration buffer is labelled as the user's value. A sourced scenario remains an illustration, not an accepted real evaluation or full feasibility result.
