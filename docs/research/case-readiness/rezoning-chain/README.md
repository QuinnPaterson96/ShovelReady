Shared retained context: Schedule M §1 permits one garden suite, restricts the
principal use/secondary suite, and allows accessory-building conversion only when
constructed before July 11, 2019 **or** at least five years before conversion;
§1(e) uses occupancy-permit issuance as construction date. Existing-building
labels do not prove that date. §2(e) requires rear-yard location; §3(b) separately
refers total-site coverage to the zone. Schedule A underlined definitions and
applicable general regulations remain dependencies. The R2-65 substitution must
be read with these provisions, not erased because the principal use is a duplex.
Schedule C parking and the rest of zoning/form-and-character requirements are
outside the two arithmetic candidates but not deemed satisfied. The source's
conditions survive any future narrow projection.

The current Schedule A download (`RC-A`) narrows the definition review: p16
places the rear yard across the lot's full width between principal building and
rear lot line; the latter is opposite/farthest from the front, with an intersecting
side-line exception. Page 18 measures setback horizontally by the shortest
boundary-to-building-face distance, excluding cornice, retaining wall, fence and
wall treatments/insulation/rainscreen up to 0.13 m. Page 19 defines site coverage
using structures and lot area; it does not itself resolve the rear-yard-specific
ratio. Page 21's yard definition allows specified ordinary projections, chimneys
and fire escapes. The selected diagrams were visually inspected; general §37
(`RC-GENERAL`, p6) makes diagrams illustrative and text controlling. These are
current-copy observations, not accepted 2024 definitions. The coverage denominator
in the table above is the proposed interpretation needing review. General §§14,
27, 38-41 retain incorporated rules, irregular-lot interpretation, cross-zone
use interpretation, conflicts and outdoor-feature qualifications. No general
eave exclusion or automatically applicable principal-building projection allowance
is inferred for this accessory unit. The consolidation includes post-cutoff changes.

The ordinary unvaried 25% route and the conditional 28.10% route are separate
legal states, not interchangeable optimistic limits. The selected comparison is
the reported post-decision route; the earlier number is retained to explain the
change, not as a second approved alternative. No relocation search is performed.
