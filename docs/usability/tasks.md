# Goal-based rehearsal script · v1

This is preparation for later testing with actual or likely users. All examples are
fictional. Do not use them for a real enquiry. The roles below are hypotheses, not
recruited participants. Give participants this page and the running preview only;
keep the answer rubric separate. Do not explain result labels before the tasks.

Ask participants to describe what they can establish, what they cannot establish,
and what visible information they used. Avoid hints about navigation or expected
answers. For later human sessions record actual observations and obtain appropriate
consent before recording; no outreach or human session was performed for this PR.

## Hypothesized manufacturer qualification coordinator

1. You are preparing a preliminary enquiry note for synthetic example A. Decide
   what you could responsibly say about proceeding, and what still needs checking.
2. For synthetic example B, prepare the next information request needed to continue
   the investigation. Explain why each requested item matters.
3. For synthetic example C, decide whether the enquiry should be abandoned or what
   further investigation could be considered. Support your note using the preview.

## Hypothesized technical reviewer

4. For synthetic example D, explain what the result establishes about this site and
   what you would need before drawing a zoning conclusion.
5. For synthetic example E, identify the outstanding dependency and explain whether
   an approval conclusion is supported. Trace the information to its retained source.
6. A colleague has an earlier note for synthetic example F. Explain whether its
   conclusion still represents the corrected saved result. Identify the inputs,
   rule, release and source dates you would cite for each version.

## Facilitator boundaries

Do not score speed, satisfaction, hesitation, demand or legal accuracy from an agent
rehearsal. Distinguish visible UI defects from hypotheses requiring human testing.
The author may rehearse visible tasks but cannot provide an independent first-user
validation; disclose that overlap. Record unavailable interactions as unverified.
