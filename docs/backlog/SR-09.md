# SR-09: Import pilot parcels and preserve zoning intersections

Status: Ready - licensed spatial intake and persistence dependencies merged (wave 1 integration: 2026-09-24)
Milestone: M1: Technical proof
Dependencies: SR-05, SR-08

## Outcome

Attach real sites to the correct source zoning without hiding split zoning or geometric uncertainty.

## Scope

Import selected municipal parcel/zoning and existing-building samples; introduce PostGIS for required spatial operations. Support manually reviewed lot-line classifications, rear-yard boundaries and proposed placements rather than automatic layout search.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Preserve original feature identifiers, geometry/CRS, source snapshots, and raw source attributes.
- [ ] Transform with explicit CRS/datum handling; verify area against independent expected fixtures with declared tolerances.
- [ ] Retain all material zoning intersections; sliver policy is documented and tested rather than silently using centroid assignment.
- [ ] Split zones, air-space/strata ambiguity, irregular sites, and missing data become investigation cases; first supported development sites are reviewed single parcels.
- [ ] Geometric area/frontage is not automatically substituted for legally defined site area or lot width.

- [ ] Verify the service schema before mapping fields. September 18 checks found Victoria OpenData_Land layers 0=Address Points, 5=folio parcels, 11=PID parcels, 1=Buildings, and 7=Contours; recheck and save metadata rather than treating IDs as permanent.
- [ ] The inspected building schema did not supply building height/year built. Missing attributes stay unknown; contour availability does not establish regulatory Average Grade.
- [ ] Inspect actual geometries for nulls, validity, currency and usable accuracy; resolve principal-building identity explicitly. Never infer elevation/slope from a two-dimensional polygon.
- [ ] Test address/parcel one-to-many joins and CRS handling; do not classify front lot lines from a longest-edge heuristic or equate parcel-minus-buildings with legal rear yard.
- [ ] Store manually supplied geometry and classification evidence with review status. Unverified heritage/secondary-suite flags must not become blanket prohibitions.

## Out of scope

No provincewide import, mandatory ParcelMap BC join, automated parcel assembly, or universal legal lot-line algorithm.

## Verification evidence

Repeatable import plus ordinary, split-zone, CRS, and missing-data fixtures.

## Open decisions and constraints

Municipal edit time does not establish a zoning provision's legal effective date.
