# SR-18: Resolve provider buy-versus-build questions with actual samples

Status: Deferred (planning status, not implementation progress)
Milestone: M0: Scope
Dependencies: None

## Outcome

Preserve the option to buy suitable data without making vendor access a prototype dependency.

## Scope

Use the commissioned provider study; prioritize vendors with actual BC samples and plausible external-product rights.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Separate verified payloads, advertisements, assumptions, and unanswered questions; inspect the same small BC cases where samples exist.
- [ ] Map coverage, measurement semantics, conditions, provenance, versions, and corrections against SR-04 needs.
- [ ] Obtain or draft precise questions for written caching/derived-output/retention/OEM rights, pricing, and termination conditions; public docs are not assumed entitlement.
- [ ] Compare remaining review effort and total cost with official-source ingestion; recommend adopt/trial/defer with reversal criteria.
- [ ] Record unverified access as a blocker for that option, not for the official-source prototype.

- [ ] Keep official-source acquisition as the prototype default; no immediate BuildData adoption follows from the reports. Obtain actual relevant samples and explicit rights evidence before recommending a dependency.
- [ ] Missing coverage listings mean unverified coverage, not confirmed absence. Distinguish geocoding from coordinate-to-zone lookup and internal caching from external redistribution.
- [ ] Separate spatial acquisition costs from regulatory interpretation/maintenance costs; do not credit a labels/polygons service with replacing rule curation.
- [ ] Report 14 duplicates report 9 byte-for-byte and is not independent corroboration. Reports 12/14 conflict; resolve claims with direct sources or precise unanswered provider questions.

## Out of scope

No purchases, acceptance of terms, provider outreach without authorization, or compulsory integration.

## Verification evidence

Evidence matrix, sample mapping, and bounded sourcing decision.

## Open decisions and constraints

Can be deferred without blocking M1; do not duplicate the already commissioned study.
