## Baseline observations, categorized

- **Observed behavior / issue targets:** the saved example automatically reached “Observed placement conflict”; the visible result reported 0.7 m² outside and 1.6 m² roofline overlap. The approximate setback panel then reported zero coherent scenarios, while the separate legal-basis candidate panel reported zero candidate checks meeting assumptions, zero apparent conflicts and four unknown/unsupported. It also listed missing front setback, rear-yard location, rear-yard occupancy, height and other applicable provisions. This is a measured conflict for the supplied rectangle and captured outlines, not an entire parcel verdict.
- **Confusing copy / hierarchy:** “Approximate setback screen” and “Legal-basis candidate checklist” compete for the result headline. The four unknown checks and source explanations require opening *Inspect candidate checks and exact evidence*; the default result does not provide one plain next action. This is the presentation problem #242/#243 intend to address, not evidence that an unimplemented candidate regressed.
- **Missing source or rule coverage:** legal lot, current zoning/bylaw, building selection/type, classified parcel edges, separation endpoints, regulatory floor-area definition and the listed unimplemented provisions were unresolved in the saved example. These are real evidence/coverage gaps, not failed software checks or a finding that construction is forbidden.
- **Untested behavior:** clear geometry, unavailable/unsupported zoning, user area entry, flag focus and value retention, 390 px layout, full keyboard traversal, and the combined #242/#243 candidate. Keep each pending until an integrated revision and exact steps/results are recorded.
