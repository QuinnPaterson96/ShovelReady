# Participant brief — give this page only

Imagine you are helping a homeowner prepare a conversation about an aux box Model 300 at **1170 MAY ST, Victoria**. There is no survey, approved placement or independently measured building height. Use the supplied ShovelReady browser page to prepare a preliminary record for that conversation.

Find the site and model, leave facts you do not know unknown, and add a note about something that needs verification. Inspect the preparation summary and its provider-discussion record. Return to the inputs, amend your note, and inspect the updated summary. Then tell us, in your own words:

1. What, if anything, did the application check for this property and model?
2. What could you responsibly take into a provider discussion?
3. What evidence or answers would you seek next, and from whom?

Please work as you normally would and say what you are looking for or find confusing. You may stop if you cannot proceed. Do not contact a provider, send the record, upload files or load a synthetic example. There is no test of your zoning knowledge.
