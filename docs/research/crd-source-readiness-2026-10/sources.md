## CRD cross-municipality fallback: metadata useful, product rights limited

[Official GIS page](https://www.crd.ca/government-administration/data-documents/maps-geospatial-data) supplies exact services and points municipal zoning to municipalities, legal property boundaries to LTSA, roads/boundaries to Province. It requires adjacent credit **Used with permission from the Capital Regional District.** It also restricts renting, selling, distribution, transfer and granting rights; public availability is not an OGL grant. Commercial app delivery, cached features, derivatives and third-party interests require a dataset-specific answer before reuse.

* [Properties/MapServer/0](https://mapservices.crd.bc.ca/arcgis/rest/services/Properties/MapServer/0) civic points: `FullAddress`, `Folio`, `AddrAuthority`, GlobalID; [layer 3](https://mapservices.crd.bc.ca/arcgis/rest/services/Properties/MapServer/3) parcel polygons: `GISLink`, `TempestLegalID`, PID, Folio, GlobalID, geometry-source/accuracy fields. EPSG:26910, max 3,000, pagination. Field coexistence is not a verified Folio/PID cardinality or stable join.
* [Basemap/Basemap/MapServer/1](https://mapservices.crd.bc.ca/arcgis/rest/services/Basemap/Basemap/MapServer/1) footprints: `SOURCE`, `DATE`, `DATACUSTODIAN`, geometry, OBJECTID; description mentions mixed 2004, Langford 2007 and Saanich 2010 inputs. [Layer 2](https://mapservices.crd.bc.ca/arcgis/rest/services/Basemap/Basemap/MapServer/2) roads: DRA centreline, transport ID/name, monthly provenance description. EPSG:26910, max 1,000, pagination. No feature samples retained; neither service supplies verified current wall geometry or delivery clearance.

CRD imagery page lists 2023 acquisition May 19–June 7; newer imagery availability does not update footprint capture dates or confer orthophoto rights. No CRD fallback is proposed for this slice until terms and source currency are resolved.
