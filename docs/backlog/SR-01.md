# SR-01: Select pilot workflow, real design, and municipality

Status: Partial - captured spatial observations imported; controlled design/site/reviewer gates remain (wave 2 integration: 2026-09-24)
Milestone: M0: Scope
Dependencies: None

## Outcome

Choose a tractable customer decision and actual inputs before committing to a geography or building type.

## Scope

Use City of Victoria garden suites as the leading technical candidate, one fixed small model (The Landing initially), and about three real sites with manually reviewed proposed placements. Vancouver is the fallback. Timebox initial source/design verification to two working days; report concrete blockers.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] One decision note identifies user, workflow, design revision, municipality, pathway, supported checks, and scope exclusions.
- [x] Actual input samples and primary URLs are recorded; missing dimensions are explicitly unknown.
- [x] Compare customer/reviewer access, data usability, rule complexity, and reuse conditions; do not infer absence of competition.
- [ ] Identify a reference reviewer and distinguish provisional interpretations from reviewed ones. Record reversal criteria if the design or pathway proves unsuitable.

- [ ] Confirm the governing current instrument per site; old Schedule M and plus-site assumptions are not accepted defaults.
- [x] Record design height/roof, installation/foundation assumptions, projections, and floor-area basis as verified or missing. A missing input is not permission to invent it.
- [ ] Customer interviews, willingness-to-pay evidence, and pilot recruitment are post-prototype work in SR-16, not prerequisites for the technical build.
- [ ] If The Landing is unsuitable, consider another documented small model before rejecting Victoria. Keep independent rule review distinct from commercial validation.

## Out of scope

No citywide ingestion, hypothetical dimensions presented as real, or compulsory six-storey DASH choice.

## Verification evidence

Reviewable decision note and source/sample inventory.

## Open decisions and constraints

Manufacturer address qualification remains a workflow hypothesis. Victoria is the leading technical candidate, conditional on actual inputs and reviewed rule applicability; Vancouver is the fallback. Scaffold and CI work need not await this decision.
