## Outcome

Use one virtual participant to test whether the current interface supports a bounded
preliminary investigation of existing lead VIC-080 (`site-80` in source identifiers).
Validate navigation, evidence retrieval and communication of uncertainty. This is an
agent rehearsal, not evidence of human usability, demand, legal accuracy or actual fit.

## Participant brief (give only this and the running URL to the participant)

You are preparing a preliminary qualification note for an enquiry associated with
VIC-080 / site-80. Use the application to investigate the lead. Explain what you could
responsibly tell a colleague, what additional information you would request, and which
visible evidence supports your note. Include enough identifying information for the
colleague to reproduce your investigation. Use the browser only. Do not inspect source
code, hidden fixtures, evaluator outputs or an answer rubric. Do not submit or modify
anything outside the local application. Report any obstacle you actually encounter.
