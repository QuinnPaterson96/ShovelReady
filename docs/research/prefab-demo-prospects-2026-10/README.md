## Two complementary onboarding choices

For an **observational model demo**, use **C.H. Studio Pod as the compact body-dimension baseline** and **Quadra 4 as the contrasting square model**. Their published body widths are 8 ft and 20 ft 1 in respectively; Quadra's awnings and WCCH's order flow test different specification and enquiry patterns. WCCH's general reservation height choices still need model-specific confirmation. Keep interior area and installed height unresolved. Do not describe WCCH as an approved permanent dwelling.

For a demo marketed specifically around **permanent garden-suite enquiries**, substitute **Nexus Yarrow for WCCH**, pending provider confirmation of its residential configuration. Its 15 ft-wide rectangle and separately drawn deck complement Quadra's square body. This is the preferable residential pair after those facts are obtained. None of the three supports an accepted height/zoning result today.

Keep the existing aux box Model 300 demo. Model 240 is solely a **same-manufacturer technical control**, not a ninth prospect or evidence of a new commercial relationship. ORCA Jay remains valuable for exercising ambiguous area, roof choices and conflicting commercial claims, independently of its lower pitch priority.

## Smallest next decisions

Quinn should select observational versus residential-demo scope, then obtain the finalists' short factual responses in the model sheets **when outreach is separately authorized**. Request one current controlled configuration and permission to show its plan; do not request a whole catalogue initially. Only then map a reviewed model into the existing catalogue and test one user journey. Measure enquiry completeness and staff follow-up effort against the current flow with consenting users; no enquiry volumes, savings, budgets or interest are established by this research.

Remaining gaps: provider-controlled elevations/area definitions and installation scope block accepted evaluation; source/image permission limits presentation to official links; Nexus's unreadable questionnaire limits the commercial ranking; real manufacturer/customer validation is absent. Provider replies, a qualified source/site reviewer and a small owner-arranged user pilot respectively own those next steps.
