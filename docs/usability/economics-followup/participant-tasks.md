# Try the project economics flow

This short exercise tests the page, not you. Use the supplied Model 300 example;
you do not need to enter personal financial or property information. Nothing
needs to be sent to a provider. You can stop at any time.

The facilitator supplies the updated public link after checking deployment.
Please use a fresh tab and mention whether you are on a phone or desktop.

## 1. Decide what to investigate

Imagine you are considering this suite as a rental. Use the page as you normally
would to decide whether it is worth investigating further. Before changing any
numbers, tell us:

- What stood out first?
- What do you think the displayed cost and income mean?
- What would you do next, and what information, if any, is missing for that step?

## 2. Try your own assumption

For this exercise, you want to try rent of CAD 2,200 per month and a delivery/lift
allowance of CAD 19,000. Find a way to try those amounts, then return to your
project. Tell us what changed, whether the result was easy to find, and whether
your changes stayed in place. These are test inputs, not suggested market values.

## 3. Explain the result

In your own words, what does the payback number tell you? Would you expect the
annual income figure to be the money left after loan payments? What on the page
led you to that understanding?

If you decided not to explore rental income yet, show how you would continue.
Finally, name the one financial figure or explanation you would most want the
page to make more prominent.
