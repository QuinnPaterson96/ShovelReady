# Victoria occupied-lot garden-suite scouting subset

Issue [#138](https://github.com/QuinnPaterson96/ShovelReady/issues/138). Status: **agent source review and proposed interface, not independently accepted rules or a published dataset**. Scope is adding one new, foundation-attached dwelling in a supplied backyard placement beside an existing single-detached house or duplex. This does not assess fee-simple or strata subdivision, conversion of an existing accessory building, permit entitlement, or every applicable check.

Page references below are **one-based PDF pages**. The separate PDF's §5, pp16–17, introduces Part 3.1(28); §3, pp6–9, amends definitions; §11, p22, ties commencement to adoption. Its displayed adoption fields are blank. The consolidation lists 25-038 on 2025-10-02 and amendments through 26-060 on 2026-07-30 (PDF pp3–4), yet its front matter still says consolidated through 22-048 (PDF p2). Matching today's bytes does not settle that conflict or prove the complete current amendment chain.

The [City zoning page](https://www.victoria.ca/building-business/permits-development-construction/zoning) says Zoning Bylaw 2018 covers **most**, not all, low-density residential property; other land remains under Zoning Regulation Bylaw 80-159. Part 1.1(2)–(6), PDF pp6–7, selects the Map 1 area, VicMap zone, and overriding site-specific provisions. For a **confirmed 2018/GRD-1 lot**, Part 4.1(1)(h),(1.1)(b), PDF p30, permits Residential use and more than one building; Part 2.2 Residential and Garden Suite, PDF pp21–22, includes a garden suite and requires a building with a foundation, designed as a dwelling unit, on a lot with a single-detached dwelling or duplex. The actual legal lot, governing map/zone, existing-house use and principal-building role, existing garden-suite count, site-specific rules, variances, and transitional approvals remain **parcel facts**, not assumptions from an address lead or a polygon. Part 3.1(28)(b), PDF p27, limits a lot to one garden suite. Part 1.1(21), PDF pp9–10, preserves specified recent approvals and transition pathways. A historical Schedule M or [older-bylaw zone](https://www.victoria.ca/building-business/permits-development-construction/zoning/zoning-regulation-bylaw) must be evaluated as its own coherent pathway; none of the 2018 values below transfer to it. Existing pilot leads are unclassified until this selection is made.

The [existing four-rule candidate packet](../victoria-garden-suite/README.md) remains provisional. Its floor-area scalar is outside this polygon-led subset: Part 2.1 Floor Area, PDF p16, uses interior surfaces and inclusions/exclusions; exterior polygon area does not supply it. Height likewise needs the bylaw grade/roof method, not GIS footprint height. GRD-1 whole-lot coverage is **not selected**: Part 4.1(6), PDF p33, has dwelling-count-dependent columns (40% for fewer than three dwelling units), while Part 2.1 Lot Coverage, PDF p18, includes *all* buildings and outdoor covered areas. The occupied-house and other structures cannot be omitted to make a remaining-capacity claim. Density, minimum lot area, setbacks for the principal house, residential requirements, parking, waterfront conditions and other permits remain separate visible checks or investigations. The existence of a useful separation or yard observation does not imply they pass.

## Interface and testable assumptions

No machine-readable `RuleContent` draft is added. The current scalar-bound packet can name one distance or ratio but cannot safely encode classified legal lot lines, a rear-yard polygon, input-surface provenance, area numerator convention, competing pathways and conditional status together. The geometry engine and UI should exchange **observations first**: placement ID; source and capture date for parcel/house polygons; CRS and units; `roof_outline`/`wall_face`/`user_placement` surface labels; line-role and rear-yard assumptions; measured distance, intersection area and ratio; uncertainty; cited candidate and source snapshot. A separately labelled assumption-based comparison can then use one of the candidate thresholds. Do not mark a legal match or accepted rule revision from this document.

Small reproducible review cases for #137/#139: (1) ordinary rectangular GRD-1 lot with reviewed front/rear/side roles and surveyed wall faces; (2) corner or through lot where assumed rear line changes the yard/setback answer; (3) roof-edge separation crossing 2.4 m while the wall-face separation does not; (4) waterfront lot invoking Part 3.1(35); (5) lot still under 80-159. Each case should preserve a geometric observation even when its legal comparison is unresolved. No real parcel is classified by these examples.

**Exact review blockers:** obtain an adopted/certified 25-038 and complete post-25-038 amendment history; confirm current Part 3.1(28), Part 2 definitions, maps and relevant site-specific/transition provisions; establish each selected parcel's governing instrument, zone and legal lot-line roles; confirm existing principal use/building and existing suite count; obtain controlled wall/placement geometry and the City's separation-face and rear-yard “occupy” conventions. These block **accepted real evaluation**, not an honestly labelled opportunity observation or evidence-only demo. #136 owns captured parcel/building evidence; #137 owns geometry observations; #139 owns display; a qualified source reviewer owns legal applicability/measurement acceptance and a later data-publication owner must publish any accepted revision separately. No outreach or source acceptance was performed.

What parcel-specific map, site provisions or transition approvals would change those answers?
