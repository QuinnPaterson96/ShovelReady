Research accessed September 24, 2026 Pacific (September 25 UTC). **Provisional;
benchmark-ineligible; no accepted rules, facts or expected legal outcomes.**
This packet advances VIC-PC-002 without changing the frozen inventory. Source IDs
below resolve in [sources.json](sources.json); [verification.md](verification.md)

The strongest return is a visually traceable **2.6 m proposed separation** and a
documented reason the two application descriptions differ. The revised letter and
revision list withdraw the separation variance after moving the addition. Indexed
