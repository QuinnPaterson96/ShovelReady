# SR-16: Run and measure a real customer qualification pilot

Status: Blocked until usable prototype; simulated walkthrough does not satisfy customer validation (checkpoint: 2026-09-24)
Milestone: M2: User-tested MVP
Dependencies: SR-01, SR-06, SR-12, SR-15

## Outcome

Test whether preliminary screening saves net work for a reachable repeat user.

## Scope

After a usable prototype, recruit through user-authorized outreach and process roughly 20–30 permissioned real enquiries. Customer validation is not a gate for source acquisition, scaffolding or the technical demonstration.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Before measuring outcomes, record workflow, buyer, baseline effort, sample selection, and success/failure/inconclusive criteria.
- [ ] Measure end-to-end user verification time, misleading candidates, false exclusions, investigation share, and unresolved causes with denominators.
- [ ] Record evidence of repeat demand/payment commitment separately from positive comments; historical failed deals are not assumed to be zoning failures.
- [ ] A reviewer adjudicates consequential discrepancies; add appropriate regression cases and document remaining limitations.
- [ ] Produce a continue/narrow/change/stop recommendation, including recurring data-maintenance cost and privacy/retention handling.

- [ ] Measure net effort including supplying site facts, reviewing placement and investigating unknowns. Do not pre-fill optimistic savings or assume municipal/planner calls disappear.
- [ ] Keep commercial interviews/recruitment post-prototype as agreed; professional rule review and obtaining technical design inputs may happen earlier.

## Out of scope

No claims of production-level accuracy from a small sample or outreach without user authorization.

## Verification evidence

Anonymized case ledger, baseline comparison, user-understanding observations, and pilot decision memo.

## Open decisions and constraints

Customer validation begins after the prototype; avoid selecting only easy cases for the usefulness measurement. Missing customer commitments do not block M1.
