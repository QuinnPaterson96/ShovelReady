## Outcome

Obtain the specifically identified approved site-plan records through legitimate
public routes, or return an exact, ready-to-review acquisition request and the
documented access requirements. Stop broad case discovery.

## Targets and acceptance

- [ ] Start with Avalon BP059152 (application 2024-09-12, now reported completed),
  its approved/revised site-plan schedule and relationship to DPV00223 and the
  January 2023 drawing set. Preserve PP059811/EP081868 as separate later records;
  completed status is not a dated occupancy certificate or condition discharge.
- [ ] Pilot targets are BP055452/BP055393 site-plan revisions and their relationship
  to DPV00081. Identify the authoritative record needed to resolve Plan 5130/5230.
  Do not reuse the old principal-house endpoint after replacement without evidence.
- [ ] Use both Property Information Portal and Development Tracker before concluding
  a record is unavailable. Begin from docs/research/case-readiness/review-2026-09-25.md;
  its Avalon portal findings are already observed, not a request to rediscover them.
- [ ] Follow a bounded public plan-acquisition route, including the City's published
  MyCity Plan Search Request guidance. Record actual eligibility, owner authorization,
  costs, permitted reuse and retention requirements only where verified. Do not
  invent a fee or imply that a public report exposes approved drawings.
- [ ] If plans are accessible, identify exact revision/stamp/incorporation and
  applicable measurement labels with page/sheet locators. Use PDF visual inspection
  where needed. Keep proposed, approved, reported completed and as-built distinct.
- [ ] If access requires a submission, account, payment, consent or outreach, prepare
  the exact narrow request text, official destination/form link, permit/property
  identifiers, unresolved eligibility/cost questions and expected useful documents.
  Do not send, submit, create an account, contact anyone or pay. Deliver the draft
  rather than another generic recommendation to contact the City.
- [ ] Explain which record would unlock which bounded check or resolve which identity
  conflict. Preserve all unfilled source/definition/review gaps. No acceptance or
  fit inference from permits, listing language or the Modo offer.
- [ ] Return a concise acquisition ledger, citation metadata and actual verification.
  No third-party PDFs, images, full text or incidental personal details in Git.

## Ownership and stopping boundary

Own docs/research/case-readiness/approved-plan-acquisition/ only. The integrator owns
the source packets and central status files. Do not alter SR-34 input annotations,
runtime code or existing frozen inventories. Limit work to these two properties and
the named records; do not retry known denied endpoints without a concrete new route,
bypass controls, broaden geography or run live extraction experiments.

Finding that a supplied/private plan is required is a valid outcome if the request
is concrete and ready for the user's review. Neither task waits for completion of
the other. Deliver a PR; do not self-merge or close parent objectives.

Follow AGENTS.md, docs/quality.md and the [wave-eight handoff](wave-eight-prompts.md).
