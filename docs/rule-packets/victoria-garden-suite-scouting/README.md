## Conditions outside this subset

Part 3.1(28)(a), p27, requires rear-yard location. The Part 2.1 Front/Rear Lot Line and Rear Yard definitions, pp16 and 19, require a legally classified lot and principal building. A long-axis guess is insufficient. Part 3.1(28)(i), p28, caps garden-suite occupancy at **25% of rear-yard area**. The numerator convention and rear-yard polygon need review; parcel area is the wrong denominator. These are outstanding checks, so even all six candidate comparisons meeting would not constitute a positive bylaw finding. Height, density, lot coverage, access, permits and other applicable provisions also remain outside this bounded packet.

Part 3.1(35), p28, applies principal-building setbacks to certain structures on specified waterfront residential lots when placed between the principal building and waterfront boundary. Part 4.1(1.1)(c), p30, restricts waterfront building location. Waterfront status and relative placement must be established; if triggered, ordinary side/rear figures cannot be treated as exhaustive. Part 3.1(28)(c), p27, has an age condition for accessory-building conversion; the packet covers a *new* suite only. Part 1.1(21), pp9–10, addresses certain transition permits/applications. A site-specific provision or variance likewise requires separate review.

## Decision and next actions

The value, operator and defined measurement basis of the **0.6 m side/rear**, **3.5 m flanking**, **56 m² floor-area** and **one-suite** candidates are source traceable. They can support explicitly labelled comparisons when matching site/model inputs and the narrow pathway assumptions are supplied. Separation awaits a reviewed endpoint convention. Missing roles, wall faces or floor-area definition produce *needs information*, not a pass. An identified exception outside this packet produces *unsupported*. Source text confidence, applicability confidence and input quality remain distinct.

For the demo, the integration worker can expose selected comparisons under assumptions and show outstanding checks. For real accepted evaluation, a site reviewer must confirm legal boundaries, current zoning, building use, existing suites, waterfront/approval facts and controlled design measurements. A qualified source reviewer must resolve the amendment/effective-text discrepancy and interpret cross-references and measurement conventions. A data-publication owner must then publish any accepted revisions separately. No human source approval, City confirmation, dataset publication or user validation occurred here.
