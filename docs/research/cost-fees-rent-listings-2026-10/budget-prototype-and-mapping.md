# Editable budget prototype and minimal data mapping

**Proposal only.** A local worksheet/review artifact can test usefulness before an application or schema change. It extends the existing selected model and unsent enquiry; it does not alter geometric/regulatory evaluation, publish catalogue facts, or introduce a financial platform. Evidence checked October 16, 2026; see [sources](sources.md) for rights, applicability and date limits.

## Cost-source decision

The strongest initial source is a builder-approved configuration/inclusion sheet entered manually, or a user-supplied written quote they may use. That source is not available yet. A public advertised starting price is a useful **priced component**, with different status and uncertainty. Broad manufacturer averages can inform questions but are a poor substitute for quotes on a constrained urban site. No inspected source provides a validated, licensed, maintained API covering the complete installed cost of these models in all four municipalities.

Use the two viable provider examples A1/A3 to validate scope, not to rank their value per square foot: size/area definitions, finishes, mechanical systems and responsibility boundaries differ. The Altus guide is a secondary professional comparator pending actual category/method/geography and licence verification; no Altus cost rate is adopted. Generic large-house rates should not be multiplied by a small manufacturer's living-area figure. A retained old price should remain visible as old, never silently escalated to today using an unrelated index.

## Minimum inputs for a useful project range

| Input supplied or reviewed | Why it changes the estimate | Who can establish it / what may remain unknown at first enquiry |
|---|---|---|
| Municipality and target timing; existing/proposed dwelling use and count | Selects authority, charging date and applicable project scenario | Owner plus municipal reviewer. Address lookup alone doesn't determine use or fee exemptions |
| Exact model/configuration and controlled revision if available; chosen finishes and energy/mechanical requirements | Identifies the price/inclusions being compared | Manufacturer; unknown design revision stays unknown |
| Factory scope versus delivery/set scope; supplier and contractor responsibilities | Prevents gaps or double counting a bundled delivery/crane/connection | Manufacturer and general contractor; reconcile in writing |
| Delivery route, access/parking, crane reach/load, overhead obstructions and street occupation | Determines transport/lift method and whether catalogue delivery is possible | Delivery/crane planner; site visit/lift plan or route survey may be needed |
| Foundation/anchoring approach, ground levels/slope, soil/rock, excavation and drainage assumptions | Separates a flat-lot benchmark from site-specific ground work | Qualified local contractor/engineer; geotechnical information may be needed |
| Existing water/sewer/storm/electrical capacity; required upgrades and trench distances; septic if applicable | Main potential scope/cost gaps cannot be priced from a map alone | Utility, municipal engineering and qualified trades; service records/design/quote required |
| Permit valuation and application date/status, development overlays, surveys/design/professional responsibilities | Supports a reviewed fee subset and soft-cost quote | Municipal reviewer/designer; unpriced until responsibility is clear |
| Tax basis, quote expiry/escalation, exclusions, contingency basis and owner-supplied works | Prevents inflated precision or an incomplete tax-inclusive total | Supplier/cost reviewer; no tax/contingency percentage default justified here |

For an early enquiry, an owner can provide intent, rough timing, configuration preferences, access photos they own and known service information; do not require a full survey before making first contact. A credible numeric range requires someone to bound the material unknown lines. Otherwise the useful result is the incomplete scope and a prioritized quotation request.

## Coverage ledger for the capital budget

Each line has exactly one cost owner and an explicit relationship to any package that includes it. “Included in another line” requires supporting scope; it is not a zero-cost assertion. Add a component once. A site-work package and its foundation/utilities breakdown are alternative levels of detail, not additive costs. Keep incompatible models, foundation methods and delivery alternatives in separate scenarios.

| Capital category | Strongest initial evidence and boundary | Initial state / update trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Factory building and selected upgrades | Named-model offer or current configuration-specific quote, itemized included finishes/systems | Published starting price or quote, never generic installed price. Recheck before sharing/expiry/configuration edit |
| Transport, route permits, ferry where required | Carrier/manufacturer route quote | Unknown until route/model/date confirmed; an Island factory does not prove zero freight/ferry cost |
| Crane, lift/set, anchoring and access works | Crane/manufacturer quote and lift/access scope | Unknown; clarify traffic control, street permits, mobilization and included anchoring |
| Foundation and excavation/site preparation | Design and local contractor scope | Unknown; identify rock, grading, retaining, drainage, disposal and restoration exclusions |
| On-site service trenches and final trade connections | Contractor/trade scope | Unknown; split private work from authority invoices; do not add again if included in a package |
| Authority connections/upgrades | Current utility/municipal estimate, not proximity of a mapped pipe | Unknown; mark minimum published tariff separately from a complete quote |
| Design/engineering/survey/energy work | Project-specific professional proposal; permit-document scope from supplier | Unknown; manufacturer drawings are not a site-foundation/service design |
| Permits/development-related charges | Individually reviewed applicability and dated formula, or actual authority fee summary | Unknown or conditional calculated component; application credit belongs to fee, security to cash flow |
| Taxes | Explicit source tax-inclusive/exclusive/embedded basis | Unknown; avoid adding PST/GST to a tax-inclusive package or assuming tax treatment of manufactured-building contracts |
| Contingency | Builder/owner scenario allowance with stated covered base and exclusions | Unset until supplied. No evidence supports a default percentage or quantile here |

The ledger is a proposal based on observed source omissions and tariff distinctions; it does not assert that every listed item applies. If a reviewer says not applicable, retain why and by whom. If only an upper allowance is unknown, the total upper bound remains unknown. A “contingency” cannot convert unbounded missing services into a reliable range.

## Concrete low-cost prototype, no integration initially

**Scope:** one local budget draft for the existing Model300 selection, usable in a facilitated review; a second blank alternative for a builder-supplied quote. Approximately 1–2 contributor-days is a planning estimate, excluding actual builder review. Use a manual worksheet/plain form or a reviewable local document; no paid tool, listings feed, scraper, background refresh, database or deployment is required. Do not build it automatically from this research.

1. Select the existing model/site and intended use. Display a separate budget status, **“Incomplete: installed range unavailable.”** Save model selection identity and capture dates without implying the budget facts were published into the catalogue.
2. Populate only the source examples below. Keep other line amounts empty; label what evidence/owner is needed. Allow an owner to enter their own range, basis and date and distinguish it from published prices and quotes.
3. If a builder supplies bounds for all material applicable lines, calculate a scenario low/high by summing non-overlapping bounds. This is interval arithmetic on chosen assumptions, not a probability interval or quote guarantee. Retain unknowns and prevent a “complete” total when coverage is incomplete.
4. Show budgeted capital, refundable securities/other temporary cash needs and funding separately. Copy a short unsent scope enquiry; keep the full source/assumption record separately available. Re-open/reconfirm after source expiry, selection or material scope changes.

### Source-only populated example

No real address, customer, contracted scope or quote is invented.

| Entry | Value / status | Source and what it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Model300 factory component | **CAD187,000, advertised starting price**; no upper bound | [aux box model page](https://www.auxbox.ca/model-300), captured October 16, 2026; manufacturer revision/price-effective date unspecified |
| Configured upgrades, site work, freight/lift, professional work, services, fees, tax and contingency | **Unpriced / unconfirmed** | Each needs its own source or explicit user/builder assumption; no synthetic cost range supplied |
| Whole installed project range | **Unavailable** | A starting price plus missing lines cannot yield a full low/high range. A priced-components figure must not be labelled “your budget” or “total cost” |
| Independent rental reference, not a project fact | **CAD2,040/month**, Victoria CMA apartment 1-bedroom asking average, Q2 2026 | [StatCan table](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=4610009201), successfully sampled; [raw evidence](statcan-sample.json). No ADU claim |
| Owner's chosen rent scenario | **Unset** | User must affirmatively enter/adopt a value; don't preselect the statistical reference as achievable rent |

The [fee worked example](municipal-fees.md#saanich-application-credit-is-essential) is independent arithmetic with an assumed municipal valuation. It is not inserted into this Model300 budget: a unit offer does not establish permit valuation, municipality or final scope.

### Keep six financial questions separate

| Question | Prototype treatment |
|---|---|
| Capital cost | Non-overlapping installed-scope lines and unresolved amount bounds; land acquisition separate if the user is buying |
| Financing | Optional user/lender terms, draw timing, interest/fees and repayment; no loan eligibility, approval or recommended financing product inferred |
| Operating expenses | Owner-entered maintenance, utilities paid by owner, insurance, incremental tax, management and reserves, with period/basis. Unknown is not zero |
| Gross rental scenario | User-chosen monthly rent × occupied months, clearly labelled scenario. Concessions/lease-up and utility inclusion need explicit treatment; no default occupancy/vacancy rate justified here |
| Net rental/cash flow | Rental receipts minus defined operating costs; debt service shown separately. Do not call gross rent “profit,” debt-inclusive cash flow “NOI,” or compute a meaningful return when costs are missing |
| Resale/appraisal | No uplift assumed; separate appraisal/comparable-data task and rights. Construction cost or assessed value does not establish resale value |

For purely transparent arithmetic, a reviewer could choose the sourced apartment reference as a scenario and explicitly assume12 paid months: $2,040×12=$24,480 **gross**. That number remains an adopted hypothetical scenario, with expenses, vacancy, debt, tax and ADU representativeness unresolved; it is not an income forecast. The recommended initial example leaves the owner's scenario blank.

### Unsent enquiry questions

Use only questions needed for the selected project; include actual model/use/site/timing if supplied. The first-contact text should fit the existing [quality standard](../../quality.md), rather than export this whole ledger.

- Which current configuration, drawing revision, price date and quote expiry apply, and what is the distinction between home price and complete installed scope?
- Please identify included and excluded work and who contracts, arranges and pays for transport/ferry, route approvals, crane/anchoring, foundation, services and final restoration. Is any planning retainer credited to later work?
- What access/service facts are enough for a preliminary range, and which uncertainty requires a route/lift plan, site visit, engineering or utility estimate? What cannot be bounded yet?
- Which site/design/permit documents and fees are included, who handles municipal responses, and how are taxes and owner works treated?
- Which timing statement begins at enquiry, signed contract, permit readiness or factory start? What is unbooked, conditional or outside your control?

No enquiry is sent, provider appointment made or site-specific response implied.

## Minimal proposed data mapping

Do not put budget fields in `Quantity` or silently change `Catalogue`, which currently validates `sr-40.catalogue.v1`. If usefulness is established, propose a small separate **versioned project-budget draft**, linked to the existing model selection/site/scenario export. The following are proposed records/fields, not an implemented schema or a requirement for a migration now.

| Existing object / proposed addition | Minimum content justified by observed examples | Reason / invariant |
|---|---|---|
| Existing model selection, unchanged | `modelId`, nullable manufacturer `modelRevision`, `snapshotId`, configuration; existing sourced dimensions and service status | Price doesn't establish controlled geometry or local supply; hash remains technical capture identity, never a manufacturer revision |
| Budget draft identity/context | Version, draft revision, model-selection reference, site/scenario reference if confirmed, municipality/use, target timing and author role | Existing local scenario handoff is the natural join; no permanent customer/project database assumed |
| Cost line | Label; original amount or original low/high as decimal strings; original currency nullable; price basis (`starting_price`, `written_quote`, `calculated_fee`, `scenario_allowance`); quantity/unit/rate basis where real | A1 supports CAD and a starting-price status; A4 illustrates unclear currency; F5 supports a rate per valuation-thousand. Never normalize a missing currency or promote starting price to fixed quote |
| Scope and applicability | Included/excluded item labels, package/parent reference, additive versus included breakdown, who supplies work, applicability state/reason, assumptions and missing inputs | Jay conflict and application credits show concrete need. No universal construction ontology; small reviewed labels suffice |
| Financial presentation | Capital/fee versus refundable security/temporary cash need; tax basis; original period/units for recurring entries | Separate deposits from cost, monthly rent from annual receipts and tariffs from works quotes |
| Source record reused conceptually | Provider/authority, public URL or private document reference, meaningful record label/section/page, capture date, revision/effective dates nullable, expiry nullable, review/access/reuse status | Preserve advertised date versus capture date versus legal effective date. Private quote attachments require permission/access handling and are not public evidence exports |
| Fee calculation evidence | Authority/fee revision, charging event/date, valuation/area/unit basis, exemption/credit conditions, explicit inputs, base/adjustment/net and unresolved interpretation | F2 waiver, F5 application credit and F12 delayed charge need reproducibility. The calculation is a plain typed supported operation, not executable source expressions |
| Rental context, separate from owner scenario | Source/table/series, original `REF_DATE`, quarter meaning, geography/code, stock/unit/bedrooms, measure, amount/currency/period, status/reliability/sample/universe as actually supplied | Sample StatCan fields are retained below. No sample size or utilities flag invented. Store missing/suppressed value as null with original symbol, not zero |
| Owner rental scenario | Chosen value/bounds, currency/period, adoption timestamp/role, occupied-month/expense assumptions and source links if adopted | A reference becomes a scenario only after explicit adoption; a scenario remains unverified |
| Timing evidence | User target distinct from dated provider statement; start/end milestones and dependencies, reservation status unknown unless evidenced | Factory duration, permit delay and occupancy are not interchangeable; reuse existing catalogue `Timing` claims; project target and booked-slot evidence remain separate |

Money can initially remain in original CAD decimal strings. Do not introduce FX, tax optimization, debt underwriting, automated valuation or cross-provider financial scoring. If converting a unit rate, retain original unit/area basis and conversion precision; provider226ft² living area and300ft² footprint cannot be substituted for legal fee area or another provider's area label. Rendering may round currency for readability, but calculations and exports retain exact source amounts and supported fee rounding. Source review and publication remain separate gates.
