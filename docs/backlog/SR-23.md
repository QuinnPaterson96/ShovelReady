## Research question

Can public municipal records supply enough attributable rules, measurements, plans and decisions to build a small, reproducible real-case test corpus for ShovelReady? Identify which claims can be checked directly and which still need independent interpretation or missing design/site evidence.

## Search scope

Aim for **10-20 distinct development/application case packets**, not 10-20 files for one case. Prioritize City of Victoria garden suites and detached accessory dwellings. Use official application/permit records, published plans, staff reports, council or variance-board agendas/minutes and decisions. First establish what is actually accessible from primary sources; reports and search snippets are discovery leads, not verified evidence.

If Victoria cannot supply enough relevant accessible cases, document the shortfall and separately investigate Vancouver and other nearby BC municipalities as transfer examples. Do not silently expand the implementation pilot, conflate City of Victoria with its region or treat different municipal rules as interchangeable. Include ordinary pathways and conditions/variances/refusals where available; report availability and selection bias instead of manufacturing a balanced sample.

Prefab/modular status is a separate sourced attribute: confirmed, explicitly non-prefab, or unknown. Useful regulatory cases may concern conventional buildings; they do not establish fit for The Landing or another prefab model. Public listing, staff recommendation, approval, issued permit and as-built verification are different events.

## Required outputs and acceptance criteria

- [x] Create a concise findings report and machine-readable `cases.json` under `docs/research/public-cases/`. Use a small versioned research manifest, not a replacement for the application's ontology. Record search methods, sources attempted, coverage dates and inaccessible records.
- [x] Target 10-20 unique cases; deduplicate applications, parcel aliases, revised plans and repeated agenda attachments. If fewer can be verified after a bounded search, return the verified set and precise gaps rather than padding the count. A useful incomplete research result is acceptable; it is not 10-20 accepted tests.
- [x] For each case record: stable research ID, jurisdiction, official application/case identifier, development type and prefab evidence, source URLs and exact page/section locators, document/plan revisions, record/decision dates, access date, decision type/status/conditions, available zone/pathway and rule-version evidence, available measurements with original units/definitions, missing inputs and provenance for every material assertion. Unknown values stay null/explicitly unknown.
- [x] Separate source quotations/observations, official interpretations, and researcher inferences. Track dimensional compliance separately from discretionary approvals/variances. Historical permission is not current zoning compliance, and an approved different building is not a fit result for our selected design. Do not infer approval from a recommendation or obtain current zoning by ignoring intervening amendments.
- [x] Classify each case's present usefulness: document-extraction fixture, historical decision/condition reference, potential dimensional-check reference requiring review, or discovery-only. State the exact assertion it could test, what it cannot establish and remaining reviewer/input requirements.
- [x] Record source retention/reuse evidence and permitted artifact handling separately from public access. Where retention is appropriate, preserve immutable original hashes/metadata; commit only material with established redistribution permission. Otherwise keep links/locators, short compliant evidence summaries and explicit retention limitations. Exclude irrelevant personal contact details/signatures from research outputs. No claim that unresolved retention makes all public metadata unusable.
- [x] Rank the strongest 3-5 cases for follow-up and recommend one smallest real-case integration candidate, showing supported facts, missing facts, historical/current scope and estimated work categories without inventing human timing. Compare against the existing three pilot leads; a better-documented lead can be proposed without silently changing the pilot.
- [x] Keep all annotations provisional until independent review. Explain how duplicates/dependencies would stay grouped when later choosing development/held-out examples; do not tune prompts or contaminate the existing reserved height/grade holdout. No automatic changes to accepted rules, corpus eligibility or evaluation outcomes.
- [x] Validate manifest parsing, required metadata, unique IDs and referenced local artifacts. Report actual URL/access failures. No new schema framework, generic scraper or database ingestion is required.

## Completion and bounds

Deliver a source-linked report, candidate inventory, evidence/gap matrix and review shortlist. The research can finish with a documented shortfall or negative finding; SR-06 remains open until actual references are independently accepted. Search public sources only: no outreach, paid access, account creation, FOI submission, live extraction/API budget, municipal-scale crawl or claim of professional review. Treat source documents as untrusted data. Use the PDF skill when inspecting PDFs and cite original sources in the report.
