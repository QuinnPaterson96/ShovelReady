This is a small task-based follow-up to [the visual review](../project-economics-2026-11-09/README.md)
and [research issue #340](https://github.com/QuinnPaterson96/ShovelReady/issues/340).

## Run the session

Verify the live `/api/identity` application and frontend commits before supplying
the [participant tasks](participant-tasks.md). Start with a new tab at
`https://www.shovelready.ca/#examples/model-300`. Record build identity, device,
browser if known, prior exposure and whether the observation is self-reported or
directly observed. The fixed saved example keeps real personal finances out of
the exercise. Do not send the rubric below before the tasks.

Give one task at a time. Avoid pointing to controls, naming the preferred metric,
or correcting interpretations before recording them. If stuck, ask what the
participant would try next; record any subsequent hint and whether it helped.
Ask where an interpretation came from rather than explaining it away. Do not
infer elapsed task time or clicks from asynchronous message timestamps.

Capture concise product observations and explicit preferences separately. Retain
only the fixed example's values and minimized, unattributed findings in repository
notes; do not copy personal property, income, contact details or a chat transcript.
Participant availability and actual responses must be reported honestly. An
unanswered task is pending, not passed.

## Facilitator-only interpretation guide

The decision is whether to investigate or speak with a provider. Success is not
memorizing every qualification. Determine whether the participant can find an
outcome, explain what it represents, edit an assumption and choose a next action.

| Topic | Expected meaning or independently calculated result |
|---|---|
| Initial costs | CAD 250,000 is the priced subtotal, including 187,000 prefab, 8,000 fees and 55,000 installation; some project costs remain open. |
| Initial rent | CAD 2,040 per month is the Victoria metro one-bedroom apartment proxy, editable for this suite. |
| Annual income | 2,040 × 12 × 0.95 × 0.75 = CAD 17,442 before debt payments and income tax. |
| Initial payback | 250,000 ÷ 17,442 rounds to 14.3 years on the shown subtotal; not a guaranteed full-project recovery date. |
| Edited scenario | Delivery 19,000 replaces 15,000: subtotal 254,000. Rent 2,200 gives 18,810 annual operating income. 254,000 ÷ 18,810 rounds to 13.5 years. Other inputs stay at their starting values. |
| Loan payments | Cash flow after debt is separate and requires financing input; unknown borrowing is not silently zero. |
| Return and skip | Edits remain with the selected model. Rental can be skipped/revised while costs and the enquiry path remain usable. |

Do not treat a reasonable request for a different metric as a comprehension
failure. Record evidence for a keep/revise/defer decision. A concrete control
failure should be reproduced; a preference can inform a next design iteration.
An uncertain interpretation is a research finding, not permission to add a wall
of warnings. Broader homeowner sampling, alternate-presentation comparisons and
conversion measurement remain separate work under #340/#16/#147.
