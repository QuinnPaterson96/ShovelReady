## Decision and workflow

Keep City of Victoria garden suites as the leading technical candidate and Click Modular's **The Landing** as the initial real design candidate. The exact manufactured revision/configuration is unknown. The manufacturer webpage and linked plan/elevation image dated April 18, 2024 establish a real product and some plan dimensions, not a complete controlled current design. Target a new detached installation attached to a foundation, evaluated at one supplied placement per site. No manufacturer suitability or site fit has been established.

The intended user is a prefab manufacturer's sales/site-qualification coordinator, assisted by a technical reviewer. The proposed decision is whether to advance a particular model/site/placement for further investigation and what must be checked next. Supply the design revision, site facts and placement; show the bounded checks, source evidence, missing facts and release identity. This workflow remains a product hypothesis. Commercial interviews, recruitment and willingness-to-pay validation belong after the prototype in SR-16 and do not gate this work.

The provisional pathway is Zoning Bylaw 2018 (18-072), GRD-1 residential, with the Garden Suite use definition, Part 3.1(28), Part 2 definitions and applicable Part 4/general provisions. For the three captured leads, the actual zoning responses return `ZoningBylaw=ZB2018`: site 59 is GRD-1, sites 80 and 86 are GRD-1 (PGA). This establishes the observed GIS designation, not a certified legal conclusion about the site, amendments, permit transitions or exceptions. The intended principal-building use must be verified; a roofline cannot establish a single-detached dwelling or duplex.

Propose seven initial dimensional checks, once their semantics and inputs are reviewed: garden-suite floor area, roof-dependent height, side clearance, rear clearance, flanking-street clearance where applicable, principal-building separation, and rear-yard occupancy ratio. Use, one-suite count, location in the reviewed rear yard, zone/era selection, waterfront/heritage/other restrictions, and coherent whole-lot density/coverage remain explicit applicability or unresolved checks. Do not treat the seven dimensions as comprehensive compliance. Part 3.1(30) residential provisions and Part 4.1 density/coverage/parking relationships need review; do not assume the garden-suite subsection displaces all general provisions.

Excluded from this proof: automatic placement search, universal no-fit conclusions, arbitrary address search, citywide coverage, rezoning/variance advice, building-code certification, servicing, title/covenant review, trees, geotechnical/flood/environmental feasibility, transport/crane access and permit entitlement. An exclusion from implemented coverage is not a zoning prohibition. Preserve any known restriction as an unresolved investigation item.

## Current-era evidence and reversal criteria

The [City zoning index](https://www.victoria.ca/building-business/permits-development-construction/zoning) distinguishes the geographic scope of both bylaws. The captured 2018 consolidation's Part 1.1(2)-(4), PDF p6, identifies its map area and disapplies 80-159 there. Its amendment table reports modernization 25-038 on October 2, 2025 and later amendments through July 30, 2026. Its publishing note still says June 2022; do not report that note as the current consolidation date. The separately downloaded 25-038 copy has blank adoption fields. Obtain a certified/current amendment chain before accepting legal currency. These limitations are recorded separately from the actual capture date and PDF metadata.

Do not import Schedule M's old floor-area limit, secondary-suite restriction or plus-site rules into these ZB2018 leads. Keep Schedule M only as a wrong-era regression lead; it may still apply elsewhere under its own conditions. Existing permits are not fit evidence for The Landing today.

Reconsider The Landing when dimensioned drawings establish an unresolvable mismatch or the needed configuration cannot be fixed/reused. First consider another documented small model in Victoria. Reconsider Victoria for Vancouver only if current applicable rules, required source reuse or sufficient site facts/review cannot be obtained within the SR-01 two-working-day verification timebox. This work was interrupted between September 18 and 24; it is not a claim of six days of verification or completion of that timebox. Do not reject a city because customer interviews have not occurred.
