## Material findings and issue candidates

**W13-1 — Parcel confirmation clears on note edit; first summary omitted site and note. Severity: P2 candidate, usability/workflow; not confirmed app defect.** Reproduce on the pinned build: search `1170 MAY ST`, choose `Use this parcel lead for my summary`, enter a source/uncertainty note, and press `Prepare summary` without reconfirming. Observed summary: `Site: not selected`, manual notes `none`; the note still exists in inputs. Contrary evidence: inputs explicitly warn that editing any site field clears confirmation; reconfirming the lead restores both site and note in the next summary. Practical impact: a homeowner can lose the chosen context from a copied record even though they entered a note. Next action/owner: product/UI owner to decide whether a note should invalidate parcel confirmation or whether preparation needs a more prominent recovery prompt; test with humans under #16 before prioritizing.

**W13-2 — Ordinary full Street spelling yields no exact address match. Severity: P3 coverage/usability candidate; false-positive bug claim if described as broken lookup.** Reproduce: Address search `1170 MAY STREET` returns no exact match; `1170 MAY ST` returns one retained lead. Contrary evidence: page states five captured rows, bounded exact lookup, and manual facts/PID fallback. Practical impact: an ordinary homeowner spelling can lead to a premature manual fallback. Next action/owner: address-lookup/product owner to consider alias/normalization coverage and evaluate independently; no claim that the service failed or that all Victoria addresses are supported.

**W13-3 — Copied records need editing before a provider contact. Severity: P3 usability hypothesis, not a confirmed defect.** The 3,368-character provider record preserves identifiers, snapshots, provenance and unknowns, while the expanded evidence-request record repeats many long citation lines across twelve items. At 390 px it is a narrow scrollable text area; checklist items reflow without page overflow but hashes dominate the expanded view. Contrary evidence: the first checklist rows clearly name a missing artifact, impact, supplier and next step; I could draft the exact concise enquiry in `unsent-provider-enquiry.txt` from them. Practical impact: the app supports preparation but the copied output may demand substantial filtering before a real conversation. Next action/owner: UX owner and #16 human study to test whether users can select a short provider-specific request while preserving provenance in a separate review record.
