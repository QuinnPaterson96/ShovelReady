# Facilitator script

**Before arrival (facilitator only).** Confirm the browser shows the pinned baseline identities in [README](README.md), that lookup uses the five-row address packet, and that this is a fresh draft. Record actual setup, URL, device/viewport, participant type, prior exposure and any deviations on the [sheet](recording-sheet.md). Use a participant code, not a name or contact details. Set a session-specific time limit and announce it. Do not provide the [rubric](adjudication-rubric.md), expected parcel identifier, code, API output or hidden source packet. If the service is unavailable, record a blocked attempt. No recruitment or outreach is authorized by this kit.

**Opening.** “We are testing whether this preparation page helps with a real conversation. Please think aloud. I may ask what you mean, but I will not guide you through the page. You can stop at any point.” Give the [participant brief](participant-brief.md) and the local URL. Ask them to read the task aloud or silently. Start task time when they begin interacting; note pauses and assistance separately.

**During the task.** Observe the sequence without naming controls. Allow lookup, explicit parcel-lead confirmation, manual facts and unknowns to be handled by the participant. If they ask for help, first ask “What would you try next?” If they remain stuck, offer the smallest navigation hint needed; record its exact wording, time and effect. Do not supply an area, height, placement, zoning interpretation or expected answer. Distinguish an unavailable lookup, no match, app error and browser/tool failure. Record visible evidence and quotes, not inferred intent. If they enter a value without support, ask neutrally, “Where did that value come from?”

**After their final summary.** Ask, without correcting them first: “What did the app actually check?” “Which details came from a source, and which came from you?” “What evidence would you need before deciding whether this model could work here?” “What would you take to the provider, and what would you ask the provider versus a surveyor or municipal/reviewer contact?” Then ask: “What, if anything, would you change about this page?” Record comprehension and preference separately. If they claim fit or approval, ask what on screen led them to that conclusion; do not treat the claim as a legal determination.

**Close and debrief.** Stop the task clock when they finish the record/explanation or abandon the task; record interview time separately. Explain that the page is preliminary preparation and no checks or permit decision were performed. Do not send the provider text. Save only a consented, minimized observation record under the team's approved retention process; if no process is authorized, keep the blank template and report that collection is pending. Apply the withheld rubric after the session, retaining raw observations and disagreements.
