# Next municipality for the homeowner prefab demo

Research refreshed **October 7, 2026 (America/Vancouver)**. For the ShovelReady integration owner deciding the next bounded extension. Observed means inspected source or reproduced query; inferred means a recommendation from that evidence; unknown means it was not established. This is research, not a legal feasibility opinion, accepted data release or implementation mandate. Full technical records are linked in the [source inventory](sources.md) and [verification record](verification.md).

## Decision

**Choose Saanich for the next approximate-placement demonstration. It is also the strongest strategic choice on the evidence collected**, because its municipal catalogue expressly permits commercial reuse and redistribution and supplies addresses, parcels and rooflines together. This reduces reliance on uncertain third-party rights and provides a realistic maintenance experiment. That is a data-readiness judgment; demand, conversion and manufacturer economics were not measured.

Start with one confirmed existing public case, source-attributed parcel/roof geometry and a user-supplied Model 300 placement. Keep Saanich regulatory comparisons unavailable until independently reviewed. Its known zoning defects remain. Do not treat roofline availability as a legal footprint or geocoder locality as jurisdiction. The [Saanich catalogue](https://www.saanich.ca/EN/main/local-government/data-catalogue-1.html) and [licence](https://www.saanich.ca/assets/Local~Government/Documents/SaanichDataCatalogueLicence.pdf) were inspected afresh; the licensed downloads and two joins were reacquired.

Rank **Langford second**, conditional on municipal rights and source linkage; its current bylaw expressly accommodates modular garden/carriage suites, but public map access does not establish product reuse rights. Rank **Oak Bay third for automation**, while retaining it as a useful manual ADU case: detached ADUs are a real municipal pathway, but reusable municipal GIS, current consolidated rules and overlay coverage were not verified in this session. These rankings concern extending the present homeowner flow, not relative municipal buildability.

## What changed since the earlier research

This refresh builds on [Saanich spatial/topology work](../../municipality-transfer/saanich/spatial/README.md), [Saanich rules](../../municipality-transfer/saanich/rules/README.md), [Langford rights/selection work](../../municipality-transfer/langford/spatial/rights-and-selection.md), [Langford rules](../../municipality-transfer/langford/rules/README.md) and [prefab supply research](../prefab-supply/README.md). It does not replace those packets or alter their acceptance status.

* **Saanich releases changed, not the observed defect.** Civic rows are now 32,963 (previously 32,965); parcels 42,927 (42,924); zoning remains 1,890 but archive bytes changed. Roofline archive is byte-identical, 62,320 records. Metadata cadence is not a feature currency guarantee.
* **Old source indexes cannot identify new zoning rows.** Kingsley's defective RS-6 is now row 1854 rather than 1853, with the same reported self-intersection. Cedar Hill's valid RS-10 is now 1465, while defective RS-6 is 1775 rather than 1659. Querying the old positions in the new archive returns other zones. This does not resolve [#112](https://github.com/QuinnPaterson96/ShovelReady/issues/112); retain source release plus attributes/geometry evidence, never indexes alone.
* **Langford's key limits reproduced.** Lone Oak's first-point candidate and Jenkins's failure recur. A bounded query around one point says nothing about other returned candidates or title identity. Municipal feature responses were not acquired; metadata does not supersede the [City disclaimer](https://langford.ca/disclaimer/).
* **Oak Bay adds a genuine detached-ADU lead.** Its [housing program](https://connect.oakbay.ca/housing-action-program) includes detached accessory dwellings, and [DP000067](https://onlineservice.oakbay.ca/WebApps/OurCity/Prospero/Details.aspx?folderNumber=DP000067) describes garage conversion alongside a house and secondary suite. Internal secondary-suite guidance must not be mistaken for a detached-ADU prohibition. Archived/file-completed status does not establish occupancy approval.

## Smallest slice, stop conditions and next work

**Proposed slice:** under [#248](https://github.com/QuinnPaterson96/ShovelReady/issues/248), use the existing homeowner components with one Saanich catalogue case. Confirm municipality separately, source parcel and rooflines as unreviewed observations, ask the user to identify the house, measure a supplied rectangle, and produce a short enquiry with explicit missing legal checks. Keep manual `LOCAL:METRE` fallback. No automatic placement search, regional import platform or new service is justified.

This proposal is conditional on integration ownership resolving the scope of #112's existing runtime exclusion; the research does not silently enable Saanich. Follow [#246](https://github.com/QuinnPaterson96/ShovelReady/issues/246) for jurisdiction and [#247](https://github.com/QuinnPaterson96/ShovelReady/issues/247) for definition context. Switching municipalities must invalidate legal results without relabelling physical measurement bases.

**Falsify practicality** if the bounded case cannot preserve holes/CRS and survive a fresh file release, if a user cannot reliably distinguish/confirm parcel and main house, if a needed input requires rights not supplied by the catalogue, or if the honest output does not help a homeowner prepare a manufacturer enquiry. Also stop expansion if a combined geometry summary implies zoning success. Two successful joins do not establish citywide performance; human usefulness remains unmeasured.
