# Model 240 eave clearance

The placement pilot helps someone decide whether to move the proposed suite or ask aux box for a dimension before shortlisting this position. It covers one long side of the aux box Model 240, Bath + Kitchen, on a mapped parcel. The dated Model 300 remains available through the existing journey.

## What the drawing supports

On November 19, 2026, we inspected the manufacturer's public specifications, floor plan, elevation and Bath + Kitchen layout. The plan dimensions the drawn outer sides at 9 ft 11 in and the ends at 24 ft 1 in, including the covered deck and mechanical-storage end. The product specifications give a nominal exterior width of 10 ft. The elevation provides height. None of the inspected drawings gives a horizontal wall-to-outer-gutter dimension for this installation. Drawing revision and installed edge are unknown. The catalogue rectangle retains the nominal dimensions. A homeowner can compare the chosen side with the mapped parcel boundary while the installed edge is unknown. Assuming the width ends at the wall lets the homeowner try an additional eave or gutter depth on that side. Assuming the width already includes the gutter uses the rectangle's side once. The side turns with the unit. An apparent crossing concerns the physical sketch and calls for moving the unit or confirming the dimensions.

## What would justify expanding the check

- **Provider evidence.** Ask aux box which edges its published width reaches and for the installed wall-to-outer-gutter dimension on the side under review. A controlled configuration-specific drawing could replace the assumption.
- **Planning review.** Before crediting any exception, a reviewer needs to establish the parcel's governing bylaw, the applicable feature and yard, and both measurement endpoints. Victoria's GRD-1 projection table and garden-suite provisions are separate. Mapped lot lines are preliminary. [Victoria Zoning Bylaw 2018](https://www.victoria.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-2018) · [Victoria Schedule M](https://www.victoria.ca/media/1081)
- **Homeowner and plan validation.** Product research can replay ten permission-cleared placements, including tight and roomy sites, against independently annotated plans. Record the limiting feature and next action before revealing the pilot output. The proposed expansion threshold is a changed placement or more specific drawing request in at least two cases, with no documented conflict reported clear. Homeowner sessions should also establish whether people can identify the edge and ask the provider the relevant question. These studies have not been run.
